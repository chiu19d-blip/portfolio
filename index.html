hi, i’m kanika

I’m Kanika, an independent graphic designer who turns ideas into identities, images and moving pieces. I work with brands and creators who want a clear, memorable look.

Philosophy. Good design is restrained and intentional. I start with the story, strip away what doesn’t serve it, and let contrast, typography and colour do the work.
